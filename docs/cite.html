Cite#

Cite the paper for the part you used. Each method of the package comes with its own paper.

The papers#

The reconstruction methods (FF-STEM: direct ptychography, tilt-corrected dark field and their fusion) are described in:

S. You, G. Varnavides, S. Khavnekar, N. Palatkin, S. Shao, M. Wu, D. Stroppa, D. Chernikova, B. Zhu, R. Egoavil, S. Vespucci, D. Krishnan, X. Ye, F. K. M. Schur, E. Spiecker, P. Pelz. Gap-Free Information Transfer in 4D-STEM via Fusion of Complementary Scattering Channels. Advanced Science 13 (2026) e76620. doi:10.1002/advs.76620

The STEM-EELS simulation (BiP-PRISM) and the elastic PRISM code are described in:

P. Pelz. The bi-partitioned PRISM (BiP-PRISM) algorithm for fast and scalable core-loss STEM-EELS simulations. Journal of Physics: Materials (2026). doi:10.1088/2515-7639/aebdd0

The journal has not yet given the BiP-PRISM paper a volume or an article number.

The software#

Zenodo archives every release of scatterem. The concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22162829 always resolves to the latest release. Each release also gets its own version DOI; the Zenodo record lists it. Cite the version DOI when you report numbers that depend on the release.

CITATION.cff in the repository holds the same metadata in machine-readable form. GitHub shows it as “Cite this repository”.

The data#

The FF-STEM datasets (the four specimens of the paper) are on Zenodo under 10.5281/zenodo.18008901, with the licence CC-BY-4.0. If you use them, cite the record as well as the paper.

Scope#

Three things live here, and each comes with its own paper:

  • the FF-STEM reconstruction pipeline: direct ptychography, tilt-corrected dark field, their fusion, and the calibration and I/O they need;

  • the core-loss STEM-EELS forward simulation (BiP-PRISM);

  • elastic PRISM simulation of 4D-STEM.

The wider scatterem codebase covers more. Those parts come with their own publications and are released with them.
